Profiles and activity

Lobby saves the profile details and pictures you add. People in your rooms, friend requests, friends list, or direct conversations can see your username and profile details. Friends and people in your rooms can view your profile pictures. Activity and account links appear when you turn on activity sharing. Use pictures you have permission to share and link your own accounts only.

For a live service the site has enabled, Lobby sends your linked public account identifier to that service to check current activity while you are active in a room. Lobby briefly caches the returned status and displays it in your room. You can clear account links or turn sharing off in your profile.

Friends, direct messages and plans

The app stores friend requests, friendships, blocks, direct messages and read positions to provide these features. Direct conversations are limited to their two participants through the app’s access controls. They are stored by the app and are not end-to-end encrypted. Direct messages are not sent to ChatGPT by this feature.

Blocking removes a friendship and prevents new direct messages and friend requests between those accounts. It does not hide messages in shared rooms. Room hosts and app admins can ban or unban room members.

Room events, RSVPs, checklists and announcements are visible to room members. Reminders and pending scheduled messages are visible only to the account that created them through the app. Scheduled messages become visible to the room when delivered. Delivery is checked when someone opens the room; reminders and browser notifications require the app to be open.

ChatGPT in rooms

When the host connects an OpenAI API key, room members can request a reply by mentioning @ChatGPT. A request sends recent visible message text from that channel to OpenAI, including participant display names attached to those messages. The reply appears in the channel for everyone in the room. Avoid sharing sensitive information in channels where members can request AI replies.

The person who connects the API key is responsible for charges on that API account. Get any required parent or guardian permission before using the AI feature. AI replies can be wrong, so verify important information before relying on it.
