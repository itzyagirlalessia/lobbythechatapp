Report a copyright concern

If you own the rights or are authorized to act for the rights holder, email a copyright report to hergametechnologies@gmail.com. Include the following information in your report.

  • Your name, a reply address, and whether you are the rights holder or an authorized representative.
  • A description of the original work, plus a reference or link if available.
  • The page address, or the room name, channel, approximate time, and message excerpt needed to locate the material.
  • Why you believe the use is unauthorized, along with a statement that your report is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Keep room invite links and access credentials out of your report. The site operator may ask for more information and may restrict access to the reported material while reviewing the concern.

Ask for a review or report abuse

If a message was hidden in error, email the same address with the room, channel, and reason you think it should be restored. Include any relevant permission or license. For harassment, spam, or other misuse, describe what happened and where it occurred so the operator can find it.
